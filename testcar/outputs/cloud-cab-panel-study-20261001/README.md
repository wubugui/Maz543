# Independent MAZ543A cab-panel layout study

This is an editable, **FITTED** native Blender study of the two distinct panels
in the 1977 manual, Fig101 (pp174–175) and Fig102 (p176). It is **not installed**
in either vehicle master. Production was neither opened nor modified. All 16
whole-vehicle acceptance gates remain **OPEN**.

## Native deliverable

`study.blend` is 5.8 MiB and contains 386 objects, including 247 physical proxy
solids, retained Boolean operands, three editable source curves, separate
assembly roots, three review cameras and neutral review lights. It contains
no source-image pixels, file textures, calibrated scales, simulated circuits,
drivers or animation. No render was made by this builder.

- The left panel has the original drawing's upper U-shaped cutout and asymmetric
  right/lower extension. There are 14 large instrument positions in two rows
  of seven, with separate fitted body, blank face, bezel and cover proxies
- The visible left small-control positions are represented by 32 additional
  proxies. Their unverified function assignments are deliberately left unknown
- The right panel has its own contour and nine physical positions: fan, heater,
  ceiling-light and panel-light switches; socket; two thermal fuses; large task
  lamp; driver-signal button. The large upper circle is a lamp, not a gauge
- A separate unidentified right-side strip retains its two real oblong openings
- Source curves, Solidify, Boolean, Bevel, native cylinders, torus and sphere
  primitives remain editable. No manually scripted mesh construction was used

All metric widths, thicknesses, hole centres, diameters, axial depths, display
spacing and materials are fitted study choices. The left width is 0.880 m,
right width 0.335 m and both main sheets 0.003 m thick. These are not published
MAZ dimensions, and no size calibration was transferred between the figures.
The source perspective/skew was not independently rectified.

## Source correspondence and unresolved conflict

Physical slots are named A1–A7 and B1–B7. They are kept separate from the manual
caption function IDs:

| Physical row | Drawing leaders, left to right |
|---|---|
| A1–A7 | 58, 59, 61, 63, 66, 68, 70 |
| B1–B7 | 57, 60, 62, 64, 65, 67, 69 |

The original source contains four unresolved conflicts. Drawing leader 56
points to a small control while its caption says speedometer; B1/leader57
looks speedometer-like while caption57 says air pressure; A4/leader63 looks
pressure-like while caption63 says voltammeter; B4/leader64 looks VA180-like
while caption64 says fan switch. The study records these facts separately.
It does not silently renumber or assign a final function to the affected slots.

The directly inspected supplementary VA180 photo supports an upper arc window
and lower-button visual inference for B4 only. The removed and installed panel
photos in WolfPRo's DTF article support visible device forms, but their exact
vehicle variant/year is unknown. The removed panel has a flat upper edge,
unlike Fig101's U cutout. It was not used to replace the 1977 contour. The
supplementary source register also records the later SP110-3802010 catalogue's
85 mm **case mounting diameter**, not front-bezel diameter; no 1977 compatibility
or fit was assumed here.

The source pixels remain in the local research folder. Only metadata,
observations, URLs and SHA-256 values appear in `source-registry.json` and
`../../reference/cab-panel-supplementary-20261001.json`.

## Actual three-view inspection

The separate render script reopened the saved study and produced front, oblique
and rear Cycles CPU images at 2200 × 1100, 48 samples without denoising. All
three actual images were inspected. The complete panels fit inside the frame;
the front includes diagnostic labels and the other two views do not. Source
geometry was not hidden or altered for the images, and the study SHA remained
unchanged. See `render-provenance.json` for image hashes and camera matrices.

The views establish visible layout and front/rear proxy depth only. Glass
reflections are strong and faces are intentionally uncalibrated; this is not
a factory paint, dial-printing or optical appearance pass. Rear housings lack
verified connectors, wiring and internal mechanisms. Cabin fit remains OPEN.
